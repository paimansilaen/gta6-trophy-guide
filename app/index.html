Roadmap to the platinum

Six stages, in order. Story trophies are unmissable — missions replay. Only 3 trophies are flagged missable: do those during their partner or choice windows.

  1. Stage 1 — Play the story. Chapters 1–4, the named missions and the final mission: 12 trophies, including the story's gold finale.
  2. Stage 2 — Stranger missions. Finish 8 stranger strands (Andy, Kenton, Billy, Randy, Tyrone + 3 more): 6 trophies, 1 silver.
  3. Stage 3 — Activities. Shooting range golds, races, drone races, fishing, stunts, robberies, Combat Focus and the rest: the biggest stage.
  4. Stage 4 — Collectibles & exploration. Card sets, 100 vehicles, all 52 areas, garages, Scooter Bros, the strange and the scenic.
  5. Stage 5 — Cleanup & economy. 50 challenges, laundering, spending, the partner trophies and the choice count.
  6. Stage 6 — Platinum. 100% completion, then Vice Got a Price! pops on its own.

    How this guide is built

    Trophy names, descriptions and grades are exactly as PlayStation publishes them — including the 28 hidden trophies, which this app hides behind the spoiler setting until you earn them. Every guide is written in our own words from the official descriptions, and every guide names its sources (Exophase and PSNProfiles trophy pages) beside it. GTA VI is not out yet, so where an exact location or step is not public, the guide says so honestly instead of inventing one.

    Sources: Exophase — GTA VI trophy list · PSNProfiles — GTA VI trophy list. Links open only when you follow them; the app itself fetches nothing.
